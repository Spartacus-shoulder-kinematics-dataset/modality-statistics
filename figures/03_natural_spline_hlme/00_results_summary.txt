WHY NOT cor = AR(TIME):
AR(TIME) converged (59 s) but DEGENERATE:
  AR decay cor1 = 0.000566  -> correlation exp(-cor1*dx) ~ 1 even 100 deg apart,
                           i.e. the AR process IS a random intercept.
  AR process sd cor2 = 7.139   vs random-intercept sd 6.370 without it
  random-intercept variance collapsed to 0.00065 (was 40.581)
  residual sd collapsed to 0.1039 (was 3.188)
  resid_ss kurtosis 175.2 — an ARTEFACT: resid_ss subtracts the predicted
  AR path, so it is not the eps_ij whose normality we require. 

UNMODELLED AUTOCORRELATION: within-shoulder residual lag-1 = 0.970
  A shoulder's deviation from the population curve is itself a SMOOTH
  function of x, not noise; a random intercept absorbs only its LEVEL.
  The Wald standard errors above are therefore OPTIMISTIC — treat the Wald
  counts as a descriptive summary, not as calibrated significance tests.
